Our mission

Family health transforms education

be360 is a personalised wellness accompaniment programme for families. We work on nutrition, sleep, movement, and emotional balance to generate real, sustainable change.

We partner with schools that understand healthy families are the foundation of learning. Zero cost to the institution, measurable impact for the whole educational community.

The methodology

The science of real change

We don't prescribe diets or routines. We work from each family's biology to generate lasting, sustainable change.

TTM COM-B Habit Theory Self-Directed Learning

Biological Reset System (BRS)

A clinical protocol addressing 4 pillars of balance: nutrition, sleep, movement, and mindset.

Motivational Support Component (MSC)

Behavioural layer sustaining change over time. Designed around TTM, COM-B, and Habit Theory. Validated in a clinical pilot study.

Personalised diagnosis and tracking

Each family receives an initial profile and support adapted to their context, goals, and pace of life.

Academic foundation

Grounded in Master's research in Educational Technology — University of Tartu, Estonia, 2026.

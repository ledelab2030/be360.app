What is be360?

A family wellness support program, focused on the habits that matter most: sleep, nutrition, and movement. No diets, no rigid rules. We start with a diagnosis of your child's current situation, follow with a personalized plan, and stay close by to help sustain the changes over time.

Frequently asked questions

Who is be360 for?

For families with children and teens who want to improve sleep, nutrition, and movement habits — on their own, or through their school or community.

Does it replace my pediatrician?

No. be360 complements your child's doctor — it never replaces them or interferes with ongoing treatment or medication.

How does the support work?

We first understand your child's current situation through a diagnosis. From there, we build a personalized plan that we review with you. Then, we stay close by to help sustain the changes.

If my school takes part, does it cost families anything?

No — when be360 comes through an institution, it's a benefit for families, at no additional cost to them.
